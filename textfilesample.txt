Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that enables machines to think and learn like humans.
AI can perform tasks such as problem-solving, decision-making, and language understanding.
It is used in applications like virtual assistants, self-driving cars, and recommendation systems.
Machine Learning and Deep Learning are important parts of AI.
AI helps improve efficiency, accuracy, and automation in many industries.
Today, AI is transforming healthcare, education, business, and technology around the world.
